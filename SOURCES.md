Pages were checked on 2026-11-09. Products and laws change, so check these again before you rely on them.

Labels: **Primary** is the organisation's own page or the text of a law. **Vendor** means a company describing its own product, so read it as that company's claim. **Secondary** is a third party publishing or reporting it.

## Named but not opened

The lead sources on the niche cards (licence lookups, permit records, the NPI Registry, trade associations such as ACCA, IREM, BOMA, NALP and PMPA, directories such as Thomasnet and Clutch, the Form 5500 and visa disclosure datasets, USAspending and SAM.gov, the Cyber AB marketplace) are named from general knowledge. We did not open each one or read its terms. Some may have changed, may not be public, or may forbid commercial use. See [the sending rules](docs/SENDING-RULES.md).

General statements in the notes about licensing, such as insurance agents being licensed by state, are also general knowledge and should be confirmed for your state.
