# CNC machine shops selling to engineers

**Default rank 1 of 50. Total 83 out of 100.** Manufacturing and industrial. Not in a regulated group. The idea works in most countries, though the named lead sources are often US ones.

Scores are judgment scores against [RUBRIC.md](../RUBRIC.md), not market data.

## Who the buyer is

Owner machinist of a shop with a few CNC machines who quotes jobs between setups.

## Who the booked calls are with

Mechanical engineers, buyers and purchasing managers at product companies and equipment makers.

## Why they would pay for booked calls

One good customer can fill machines for years, and a few customers leaving can hurt badly. The owner runs the floor and quotes at night, so nobody is out finding the next account.

## A ready offer

I book calls with engineers and buyers who order parts you are set up to make, so your open spindle time gets filled with work that fits your machines.

## Opening message

Replace every part in square brackets with something true you found yourself. Never send it with a detail you made up.

> [First name], the [real part, machine or capability from their site] tells me you can take on tighter work than most shops nearby. I find engineers and buyers who order parts like that and book the intro call. How much open spindle time do you have right now?

## Where to find the leads

- Thomasnet and state manufacturing directories
- Precision Machined Products Association and NTMA member lists where public
- For their customers: engineering job posts, product companies in the region and trade show exhibitor lists

## What to avoid

- Shops running full with a long backlog
- Promising tolerances or certifications the shop has not confirmed
- ITAR work unless the client confirms registration

## Compliance note

Defense related parts can fall under export control rules. Let the client decide whether to pursue that work, and do not advertise ITAR or similar status without proof. If you work from outside the US, do not accept drawings or technical data from these companies, because access by a foreign person can itself be an export problem.
