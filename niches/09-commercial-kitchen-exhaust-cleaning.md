# Commercial kitchen exhaust hood cleaning companies

**Default rank 2 of 50. Total 82 out of 100.** Commercial trades. Not in a regulated group. The idea works in most countries, though the named lead sources are often US ones.

Scores are judgment scores against [RUBRIC.md](../RUBRIC.md), not market data.

## Who the buyer is

Owner operator of a small hood cleaning crew who works nights and sells during the day when he should be sleeping.

## Who the booked calls are with

Restaurant owners, hotel kitchen managers, school food service directors and senior living kitchens.

## Why they would pay for booked calls

Restaurants need this cleaning on a repeating schedule, so every new kitchen is recurring route work. The owner works overnight and has almost no hours left to reach new accounts.

## A ready offer

I book calls with restaurant owners and kitchen managers along the routes you already run at night, so you add kitchens without adding driving.

## Opening message

Replace every part in square brackets with something true you found yourself. Never send it with a detail you made up.

> [First name], since hood cleaning happens overnight, I am guessing daytime sales is the part that gets squeezed. I can book the calls with kitchens along your existing routes while you sleep. You just show up and quote. Should I map the first 20 kitchens around [area]?

## Where to find the leads

- Google Maps for hood cleaning and exhaust cleaning
- Certification lists from IKECA where public
- For their customers: county health department restaurant permit lists and chain restaurant location pages

## What to avoid

- Telling a kitchen it is a fire hazard
- Owners who are not insured for this work
- Claiming a certificate satisfies their insurer, which only the insurer can say

## Compliance note

Local fire code and insurers set how often cleaning is needed. Let the customer's fire marshal and insurer answer that question, not your message.
