# Land surveying firms

**Default rank 6 of 50. Total 80 out of 100.** Property and built environment. Not in a regulated group. The idea works in most countries, though the named lead sources are often US ones.

Scores are judgment scores against [RUBRIC.md](../RUBRIC.md), not market data.

## Who the buyer is

Licensed surveyor who owns a small firm and spends most of the week in the field or checking plats.

## Who the booked calls are with

Civil engineers, developers, title companies and commercial real estate attorneys.

## Why they would pay for booked calls

Work comes through a small group of repeat referrers, and when one of them retires the firm feels it. The owner is licensed to do the work and has never had to sell it.

## A ready offer

I book intro calls with civil engineers, developers and title officers in your county, so you add repeat referrers rather than single jobs.

## Opening message

Replace every part in square brackets with something true you found yourself. Never send it with a detail you made up.

> [First name], I see you cover [real county or project type]. Surveying work seems to run on a few repeat referrers, so adding even one new one matters. I book intro calls with engineers, developers and title officers near you. Could I show you the names I found?

## Where to find the leads

- State board of professional surveyors licence lists
- State surveying society member directories where public
- For their customers: state engineering licence lists and local development and title company sites

## What to avoid

- Firms backlogged for months, who cannot take more work
- Claiming technical credentials for them you have not checked
- Pitching individual homeowners

## Compliance note

Surveying is a licensed profession and some states limit how licensees market. Have the licensee approve the wording.
