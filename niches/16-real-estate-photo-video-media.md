# Real estate photography and video companies

**Default rank 37 of 50. Total 71 out of 100.** Property and built environment. Not in a regulated group. The idea works in most countries, though the named lead sources are often US ones.

Scores are judgment scores against [RUBRIC.md](../RUBRIC.md), not market data.

## Who the buyer is

Owner of a small real estate media business who shoots listings and edits at night.

## Who the booked calls are with

Listing agents, team leads and brokerage marketing coordinators.

## Why they would pay for booked calls

A good agent brings repeat shoots, and every agent has been pitched by a photographer. The owner needs conversations with agents who list often, not more follows on social media.

## A ready offer

I book short calls with agents who list often in your area, starting with the ones whose current photos clearly undersell the home.

## Opening message

Replace every part in square brackets with something true you found yourself. Never send it with a detail you made up.

> [First name], your [real shoot or property] really showed the space well. I look for agents who list often in [area] and whose photos do not match their homes, and I book a short call for you. Should I send the names I would start with?

## Where to find the leads

- Google Maps and Instagram for real estate photographers
- Local photographer lists from brokerages
- For their customers: public listing portals, sorted by agents with many active listings

## What to avoid

- Criticising an agent's current photographer by name
- Using another photographer's images in the outreach
- Promising a home will sell faster
