# Trade show booth builders

**Default rank 16 of 50. Total 78 out of 100.** Business services. Not in a regulated group. The idea works in most countries, though the named lead sources are often US ones.

Scores are judgment scores against [RUBRIC.md](../RUBRIC.md), not market data.

## Who the buyer is

Owner of a small exhibit house that designs and builds custom booths.

## Who the booked calls are with

Marketing managers and owners at companies that exhibit at trade shows.

## Why they would pay for booked calls

Exhibitors are named publicly months before each event, so the timing is clear. Small exhibit houses are busy before each show and go quiet after.

## A ready offer

I book calls with marketing managers at companies booked into upcoming shows that you can serve, early enough for a real design project.

## Opening message

Replace every part in square brackets with something true you found yourself. Never send it with a detail you made up.

> [First name], your [real booth or client] shows you can do more than rental kits. Exhibitor lists come out months before a show, so I contact those marketing teams early and book the call. Which upcoming show would you most like to get work from?

## Where to find the leads

- Exhibit designer and producer association lists where public
- Google Maps and exhibit industry directories
- For their customers: public exhibitor lists on trade show websites

## What to avoid

- Contacting exhibitors right before the show when budgets are spent
- Union labor promises you cannot verify
- Claiming a booth will increase leads
