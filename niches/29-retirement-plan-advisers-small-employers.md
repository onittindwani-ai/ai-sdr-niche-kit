# Retirement plan advisers for small employers

**Default rank 45 of 50. Total 67 out of 100.** Finance and accounting. Regulated: finance. Depends on US law or US programs.

Scores are judgment scores against [RUBRIC.md](../RUBRIC.md), not market data.

## Who the buyer is

Independent adviser who specialises in 401(k) plans for small companies.

## Who the booked calls are with

Owners and CFOs of small companies that already sponsor a plan or are starting one.

## Why they would pay for booked calls

Plans are sticky and last for years, but owners seldom review their provider. Advisers are usually better at service than at finding the next plan.

## A ready offer

I book introductions with owners who sponsor a plan and have not reviewed it recently. Your compliance team approves every message before it is sent.

## Opening message

Replace every part in square brackets with something true you found yourself. Never send it with a detail you made up.

> [First name], I noticed you work with [real company size or sector] plans. Plan sponsors rarely review their setup unless someone asks them to, and that is the introduction I can arrange, with your compliance team clearing every message first. Could we talk about whether that is allowed for you?

## Where to find the leads

- SEC adviser public disclosure search, within its terms
- Firm websites and plan adviser association lists
- For their customers: US Department of Labor Form 5500 public filings

## What to avoid

- Any statement about performance or fees being too high
- Testimonials or endorsements without compliance review
- Bulk use of FINRA or SEC lookup tools against their terms

## Compliance note

Advisers and brokers face strict marketing rules. The SEC marketing rule covers advertisements and paid endorsements, including written agreements and disclosures for paid promoters. FINRA Rule 2210 bars false, exaggerated or promissory statements. A paid outreach service may count as a promoter, so the firm's compliance officer must approve the arrangement and all wording. This is not legal advice.
