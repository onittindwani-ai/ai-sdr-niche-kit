# Environmental site assessment consultants

**Default rank 14 of 50. Total 78 out of 100.** Property and built environment. Not in a regulated group. Written for the US, needs local rules and sources elsewhere.

Scores are judgment scores against [RUBRIC.md](../RUBRIC.md), not market data.

## Who the buyer is

Principal of a small environmental consulting firm that writes Phase I reports for property transactions.

## Who the booked calls are with

Commercial lenders, real estate attorneys, commercial brokers and small developers.

## Why they would pay for booked calls

Reports are ordered by a small group of repeat people such as lenders and attorneys, and the work is steady when you are on their approved list. Principals are technical and do not enjoy cold outreach.

## A ready offer

I book intro calls with commercial lenders and real estate attorneys who order site assessments, so your firm gets on their approved list.

## Opening message

Replace every part in square brackets with something true you found yourself. Never send it with a detail you made up.

> [First name], I saw your firm handles [real report type or region]. Assessments tend to go to whoever is already on a lender's approved list, and that is the introduction I set up. Want to see which lenders and attorneys near you I would contact first?

## Where to find the leads

- State environmental professional or geologist licence lists
- Consultant directories from state environmental agencies
- For their customers: community bank commercial lending pages and real estate attorney sites

## What to avoid

- Saying a site is clean or dirty before any work
- Firms with no professional liability cover
- Attorneys contacted as if for legal services

## Compliance note

Some states license environmental professionals. Avoid any statement about the condition of a specific site.
