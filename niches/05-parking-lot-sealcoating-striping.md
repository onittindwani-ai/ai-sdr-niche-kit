# Parking lot sealcoating and striping contractors

**Default rank 32 of 50. Total 73 out of 100.** Commercial trades. Not in a regulated group. The idea works in most countries, though the named lead sources are often US ones.

Scores are judgment scores against [RUBRIC.md](../RUBRIC.md), not market data.

## Who the buyer is

Owner operator who runs a small paving maintenance crew and quotes every job from his truck.

## Who the booked calls are with

Property managers, retail plaza owners, church administrators and HOA boards.

## Why they would pay for booked calls

The work is packed into warm months and the pipeline has to be full before the season opens. In winter the owner has time but no system for reaching managers.

## A ready offer

Before your season starts, I fill your calendar with site walk calls from property managers who have lots that are due for sealcoating or restriping.

## Opening message

Replace every part in square brackets with something true you found yourself. Never send it with a detail you made up.

> [First name], your [real lot or photo from their site] looks sharp. Managers usually decide on lot work before the warm months, so the winter is when I book those calls. I would contact plazas and office parks inside your drive radius. Want me to pull a first list for you?

## Where to find the leads

- Google Maps for paving and sealcoating by county
- State contractor lists where this trade is licensed
- For their customers: retail plaza listings and property manager pages, plus satellite views to spot worn lots

## What to avoid

- Starting outreach in the middle of the season when crews are already booked
- Promising a surface life in years
- Taking on an owner with no winter cash to pay for pre season work
