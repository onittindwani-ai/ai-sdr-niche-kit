# Commercial pest control for restaurants and food plants

**Default rank 33 of 50. Total 72 out of 100.** Commercial trades. Not in a regulated group. The idea works in most countries, though the named lead sources are often US ones.

Scores are judgment scores against [RUBRIC.md](../RUBRIC.md), not market data.

## Who the buyer is

Owner of an independent pest control company who wants commercial routes instead of one off home visits.

## Who the booked calls are with

Restaurant owners, multi unit operators and quality managers at small food plants.

## Why they would pay for booked calls

Commercial accounts are monthly and predictable, but independents lose them to national brands that have sales teams. A local owner needs someone doing the introductions.

## A ready offer

I book calls with restaurant groups and small food producers in your territory who want a local provider that answers the phone. You run the inspection and quote.

## Opening message

Replace every part in square brackets with something true you found yourself. Never send it with a detail you made up.

> [First name], independent shops like [their company] usually lose restaurant accounts to national brands that have sales reps, not because the work is worse. I can do the outreach to multi unit operators and small food plants near you and book the calls. Worth seeing a list for [city]?

## Where to find the leads

- State pesticide applicator and business licence lists
- Google Maps for commercial pest control
- For their customers: state restaurant association members where public, and health department permit lists

## What to avoid

- Quoting a restaurant's health inspection in the outreach
- Any claim about pesticides being safe
- National franchise locations with their own marketing rules

## Compliance note

Pesticide work is licensed and product claims are restricted. Keep messages to service and response time, and do not make safety claims about chemicals.
