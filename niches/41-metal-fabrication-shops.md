# Custom metal fabrication shops

**Default rank 4 of 50. Total 81 out of 100.** Manufacturing and industrial. Not in a regulated group. The idea works in most countries, though the named lead sources are often US ones.

Scores are judgment scores against [RUBRIC.md](../RUBRIC.md), not market data.

## Who the buyer is

Owner of a job shop doing laser cutting, bending and welding for local companies.

## Who the booked calls are with

Purchasing managers, engineers and general contractors who buy custom fabricated parts.

## Why they would pay for booked calls

Shop work is lumpy, and a few more steady accounts make payroll far easier. The owner is often also the estimator.

## A ready offer

I book intro calls with buyers at equipment makers and contractors who need a fabricator within driving distance of your shop.

## Opening message

Replace every part in square brackets with something true you found yourself. Never send it with a detail you made up.

> [First name], I saw the [real project from their site] you fabricated. Most job shops feel the swings when one big account goes quiet, so I book calls with nearby buyers who could become steady work. Could I send you a list of equipment makers within your delivery range?

## Where to find the leads

- Thomasnet and FMA member lists where public
- Google Maps for metal fabrication
- For their customers: industrial park tenant lists, machinery makers and building contractors

## What to avoid

- Quoting lead times you cannot check
- Selling welding certifications the shop does not hold
- Shops with one customer that takes all their capacity
