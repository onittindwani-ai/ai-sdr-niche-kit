## Who the buyer is

Principal of a small cybersecurity consultancy helping defense suppliers prepare for CMMC.

## Who the booked calls are with

Owners and IT leads at small manufacturers and machine shops in the defense supply chain.

## Why they would pay for booked calls

Suppliers who handle controlled defense information have to deal with this program. Engagements are substantial, and the consultants who are expert in it are not trained in outreach.

## A ready offer

I book calls with owners of defense subcontractors who need to understand where they stand on CMMC, starting with companies that have public defense awards.

## Opening message

Replace every part in square brackets with something true you found yourself. Never send it with a detail you made up.

> [First name], your [real credential or article] on CMMC is what caught my attention. Plenty of small machine shops in the defense supply chain are unsure what applies to them. I can book calls with those owners for you. Could I send a list of subcontractors near [region]?

## Where to find the leads

- The Cyber AB marketplace for registered practitioners and assessors
- Consultant websites and government contracting events
- For their customers: USAspending public award data and SAM.gov entity records

## What to avoid

- Implying a prospect is non compliant or about to lose contracts
- Saying the consultant is certified or authorized without checking the official list
- Guaranteeing a passing assessment

## Compliance note

CMMC requirements under 32 CFR Part 170 apply to defense contractors and subcontractors handling certain information on their systems. Do not give deadlines or status for a specific company, and confirm any claimed status at the official marketplace. If you work from outside the US, do not accept drawings or technical data from these companies, because access by a foreign person can itself be an export problem.
