# Regional trucking fleets seeking dedicated contracts

**Default rank 15 of 50. Total 78 out of 100.** Logistics. Not in a regulated group. Written for the US, needs local rules and sources elsewhere.

Scores are judgment scores against [RUBRIC.md](../RUBRIC.md), not market data.

## Who the buyer is

Owner of a small fleet who is tired of the spot market and wants dedicated lanes.

## Who the booked calls are with

Shipping managers and logistics leads at manufacturers, distributors and food producers.

## Why they would pay for booked calls

Dedicated freight pays more steadily than load boards. The owner is dispatching, hiring drivers and fixing trucks, so direct shippers never hear from them.

## A ready offer

I book calls with shipping managers along your regular lanes, so you can move from load boards to direct freight.

## Opening message

Replace every part in square brackets with something true you found yourself. Never send it with a detail you made up.

> [First name], I see you run [real lane or equipment type]. Direct freight rarely comes off a load board, it comes from a shipping manager who knows your name. I book those calls along your regular lanes. Which lane would you most like to fill first?

## Where to find the leads

- FMCSA carrier lookup for fleet size and authority
- State trucking association member lists where public
- For their customers: manufacturer directories and distribution centers

## What to avoid

- Promising a rate or capacity the carrier has not confirmed
- Carriers with poor safety records you can see publicly
- Freight brokers who need a different pitch

## Compliance note

Motor carriers must hold the right operating authority. Check it in FMCSA records before promoting them.
