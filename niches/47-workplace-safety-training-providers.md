# Workplace safety training providers

**Default rank 19 of 50. Total 77 out of 100.** Business services. Not in a regulated group. Depends on US law or US programs.

Scores are judgment scores against [RUBRIC.md](../RUBRIC.md), not market data.

## Who the buyer is

Owner of a small safety consulting and training business, often a former site safety manager.

## Who the booked calls are with

Safety managers, HR managers and owners at construction and manufacturing companies.

## Why they would pay for booked calls

Companies need training again as staff change, so a customer returns. Owners are usually delivering courses and have no time left to sell them.

## A ready offer

I book calls with HR and safety managers at construction and manufacturing firms near you, so your training days stay booked.

## Opening message

Replace every part in square brackets with something true you found yourself. Never send it with a detail you made up.

> [First name], with your [real background or course], I expect the training is the easy part and filling the calendar is the hard part. I can book calls with safety and HR managers near you. Would you like to see which employers I would contact first?

## Where to find the leads

- OSHA Training Institute Education Center lists and authorised trainer directories
- Google Maps for safety training and consulting
- For their customers: state contractor licence lists and manufacturing directories

## What to avoid

- Calling a course OSHA approved unless it truly is
- Naming a company's safety record in the message
- Promising fewer accidents or lower insurance costs

## Compliance note

Be careful with official sounding wording. Do not say a course is approved or certified by a government agency unless that is confirmed. Do not reference a prospect's inspection history in your outreach.
