# Small third party logistics warehouses for ecommerce brands

**Default rank 5 of 50. Total 81 out of 100.** Logistics. Not in a regulated group. The idea works in most countries, though the named lead sources are often US ones.

Scores are judgment scores against [RUBRIC.md](../RUBRIC.md), not market data.

## Who the buyer is

Owner of a small fulfilment warehouse that ships for ecommerce brands.

## Who the booked calls are with

Founders and operations leads at growing online brands.

## Why they would pay for booked calls

A brand with steady orders is steady monthly work, and every lost client leaves empty rack space. The owner runs the floor and has no sales lead.

## A ready offer

I book calls with online brands that have outgrown shipping from their own garage or that are unhappy with their current warehouse.

## Opening message

Replace every part in square brackets with something true you found yourself. Never send it with a detail you made up.

> [First name], empty rack space is expensive, and I would guess [their company] does better with brands that ship [real product type]. I look for those brands and book the call. Should I send you the first 10 I find?

## Where to find the leads

- Google Maps and ecommerce platform partner directories
- Warehouse and fulfilment marketplaces
- For their customers: online store directories, and brands with operations job posts

## What to avoid

- Promising shipping times you cannot check
- Regulated goods like supplements unless the warehouse is set up for them
- Brands too small to cover minimum fees
