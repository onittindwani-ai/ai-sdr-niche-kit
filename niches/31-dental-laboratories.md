# Independent dental laboratories

**Default rank 31 of 50. Total 73 out of 100.** Health. Regulated: health. The idea works in most countries, though the named lead sources are often US ones.

Scores are judgment scores against [RUBRIC.md](../RUBRIC.md), not market data.

## Who the buyer is

Owner and master technician of a small dental lab who still spends time at the bench.

## Who the booked calls are with

General dentists and office managers at independent practices.

## Why they would pay for booked calls

A dentist who trusts a lab sends cases every week for years. Lab owners rely on word of mouth and are losing dentists to larger or offshore labs.

## A ready offer

I book 10 minute calls with general dentists in your region who are open to a sample case from a local lab.

## Opening message

Replace every part in square brackets with something true you found yourself. Never send it with a detail you made up.

> [First name], a local lab that picks up the phone still counts for something with dentists, and your [real product or technique] looks like a good reason to try you. I can book short calls with practices in your region for a trial case. Should I send you the first few names?

## Where to find the leads

- National Association of Dental Laboratories member search where public
- Google Maps for dental laboratories
- For their customers: state dental board licence lists and the NPI Registry for practice names

## What to avoid

- Claiming a material has clearance you have not confirmed
- Any kickback, gift or rebate to a dentist for sending cases
- Handling patient scans or records

## Compliance note

Dental restorations are regulated medical devices in many countries, and some states certify or register labs. Keep to service and turnaround. Never offer anything of value for case referrals, and never touch patient data. This is not legal advice.
