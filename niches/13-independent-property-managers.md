# Independent residential property managers

**Default rank 42 of 50. Total 69 out of 100.** Property and built environment. Not in a regulated group. The idea works in most countries, though the named lead sources are often US ones.

Scores are judgment scores against [RUBRIC.md](../RUBRIC.md), not market data.

## Who the buyer is

Owner of a local property management firm with a small team, looking after rentals for individual landlords.

## Who the booked calls are with

Small landlords and owners of a few rental units who are tired of managing their own property.

## Why they would pay for booked calls

Each new landlord brings monthly management fees for years. These owners rarely market well, and their growth usually depends on referrals.

## A ready offer

I book calls with small landlords in your area who are thinking of handing over their rentals, so you can add doors without buying another portfolio.

## Opening message

Replace every part in square brackets with something true you found yourself. Never send it with a detail you made up.

> [First name], your [real neighbourhood or building detail] suggests you know this market well. Adding doors usually comes down to reaching landlords before they get fed up, and I can do that outreach and book the calls. Could I show you how I would find owners in [city]?

## Where to find the leads

- NARPM member search where public
- Google Maps for property management by city
- For their customers: county assessor records and LLC filings for small landlords, which often lack an email address

## What to avoid

- Sending anything that could be read as steering by protected class
- Emailing homeowners who are not landlords
- Buying landlord lists with unknown consent

## Compliance note

Property management is a licensed activity in many places and fair housing rules apply to how rentals are marketed. Have the client confirm their licence and approve all wording.
