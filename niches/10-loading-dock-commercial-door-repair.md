# Commercial door and loading dock repair companies

**Default rank 13 of 50. Total 78 out of 100.** Commercial trades. Not in a regulated group. The idea works in most countries, though the named lead sources are often US ones.

Scores are judgment scores against [RUBRIC.md](../RUBRIC.md), not market data.

## Who the buyer is

Owner of a dock and overhead door service company with a few vans, usually a former technician.

## Who the booked calls are with

Warehouse managers, distribution center facility leads and plant maintenance managers.

## Why they would pay for booked calls

Broken doors stop a warehouse, so the customer who already knows you calls you first. The owner needs to be that name before the next failure, and no one is working on that today.

## A ready offer

I book short calls with warehouse and plant maintenance managers so you become their first call when a dock door fails, and can quote a preventive plan.

## Opening message

Replace every part in square brackets with something true you found yourself. Never send it with a detail you made up.

> [First name], the first call a warehouse makes when a dock door jams usually goes to whoever they already know. I make sure that is you, by booking short intro calls with maintenance managers near your vans. Want to see the warehouses I would go after first?

## Where to find the leads

- Google Maps for dock equipment and commercial door repair
- Manufacturer dealer locators for door and dock brands
- For their customers: industrial park tenant lists and logistics company site pages

## What to avoid

- Dealers locked to one manufacturer's territory rules
- Promising response times the owner has not confirmed
- Sending the same pitch to home garage door companies
