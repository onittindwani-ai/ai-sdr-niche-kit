# Industrial water treatment service dealers

**Default rank 24 of 50. Total 74 out of 100.** Manufacturing and industrial. Not in a regulated group. The idea works in most countries, though the named lead sources are often US ones.

Scores are judgment scores against [RUBRIC.md](../RUBRIC.md), not market data.

## Who the buyer is

Owner of a regional water treatment company servicing boilers and cooling towers.

## Who the booked calls are with

Plant engineers and facility managers at plants, hospitals, data rooms and large buildings.

## Why they would pay for booked calls

Service accounts renew and a customer often stays for many years. Regional firms have strong technical staff and very little outreach.

## A ready offer

I book calls with plant engineers and facility managers who run boilers and cooling towers near your technicians.

## Opening message

Replace every part in square brackets with something true you found yourself. Never send it with a detail you made up.

> [First name], chemical programs tend to stay in place for years unless someone asks to look at them. I set up short calls with plant engineers and facility managers near your technicians so that someone is you. Could I share the sites I would try first?

## Where to find the leads

- Association of Water Technologies member lists where public
- Google Maps for water treatment services
- For their customers: state environmental permit lists and local manufacturers

## What to avoid

- Claiming water savings you cannot measure
- Advising on chemical handling or permits
- Implying a site has a Legionella problem

## Compliance note

Chemical handling and discharge are regulated, and health related claims about water systems are sensitive. Stick to service coverage and never suggest a site has a problem.
