# Commercial electrical contractors doing tenant fit outs

**Default rank 9 of 50. Total 79 out of 100.** Commercial trades. Not in a regulated group. The idea works in most countries, though the named lead sources are often US ones.

Scores are judgment scores against [RUBRIC.md](../RUBRIC.md), not market data.

## Who the buyer is

Master electrician who owns a small shop and bids tenant improvement work for offices, retail and clinics.

## Who the booked calls are with

General contractors' estimators, commercial tenant fit out managers and small developers.

## Why they would pay for booked calls

Getting on a general contractor's bid list is about being known to one estimator. The owner is usually on site or pricing work and rarely has a free hour to introduce himself.

## A ready offer

I get you intro calls with estimators and project managers who run tenant fit outs near you, so your shop is on their list before the next bid goes out.

## Opening message

Replace every part in square brackets with something true you found yourself. Never send it with a detail you made up.

> [First name], noticed your shop handled [real project from their site]. Getting on an estimator's bid list usually comes down to one short introduction, and I can set those up for you. I would only contact general contractors doing fit outs in [area]. Open to me sending the names first?

## Where to find the leads

- State electrical contractor licence lookups
- Local building permit records that list the electrical contractor
- For their customers: AGC and ABC chapter member directories where public, and local general contractor websites

## What to avoid

- Shops that are at full capacity and will not take a new general contractor
- Calling a bid list spot a won job
- Any firm that wants you to imply union or non union status you have not confirmed
