# ERP implementation partners for small manufacturers

**Default rank 22 of 50. Total 75 out of 100.** Technology. Not in a regulated group. The idea works in most countries, though the named lead sources are often US ones.

Scores are judgment scores against [RUBRIC.md](../RUBRIC.md), not market data.

## Who the buyer is

Founder of a small ERP consultancy that implements business software for manufacturers.

## Who the booked calls are with

Owners, plant managers and controllers at manufacturers still running on spreadsheets or old systems.

## Why they would pay for booked calls

One project is a major engagement. Partners rely on software vendor leads, and those can be inconsistent, so they want a pipeline they control.

## A ready offer

I book calls with owners and controllers at manufacturers who have outgrown their current system, focused only on the industries you already know well.

## Opening message

Replace every part in square brackets with something true you found yourself. Never send it with a detail you made up.

> [First name], your work with [real product or industry] tells me you understand shop floor reality. Vendor leads come and go, so I can build you a separate source: calls with manufacturers who have outgrown their spreadsheets. Could I show you how I would spot them?

## Where to find the leads

- ERP vendor partner directories
- Clutch and similar consultancy lists
- For their customers: state manufacturing extension partnerships, trade association member lists and job posts for ERP roles

## What to avoid

- Breaking the partner's agreement with the software vendor
- Quoting a return on investment you cannot prove
- Firms where a sales team already handles this
