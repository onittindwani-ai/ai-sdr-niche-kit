# Home inspection firms selling to real estate agents

**Default rank 23 of 50. Total 74 out of 100.** Property and built environment. Not in a regulated group. Written for the US, needs local rules and sources elsewhere.

Scores are judgment scores against [RUBRIC.md](../RUBRIC.md), not market data.

## Who the buyer is

Owner operator of a home inspection company who gets most of the work from agents who trust them.

## Who the booked calls are with

Residential real estate agents and team leads who order inspections for their buyers.

## Why they would pay for booked calls

An agent who likes your reports can send repeat work all year. Inspectors spend the day in crawl spaces and do not have time to build those relationships.

## A ready offer

I book 15 minute intro calls with active agents in your area, so they see how you write a report before their next deal needs one.

## Opening message

Replace every part in square brackets with something true you found yourself. Never send it with a detail you made up.

> [First name], agents tend to stick with an inspector whose reports are clear and calm for their buyers, and I would love to help more of them see yours. I book short intro calls with active agents near you. Want me to send the first 10 names?

## Where to find the leads

- State home inspector licence lists
- ASHI and InterNACHI inspector search
- For their customers: brokerage agent rosters and local MLS agent pages

## What to avoid

- Any deal that rewards an agent for a referral
- Promising agents a soft report
- Inspectors who are fully booked in busy months

## Compliance note

Referral payments around home sales can raise problems under real estate settlement rules, and inspector licences vary by state. Never offer an agent anything of value for sending work.
