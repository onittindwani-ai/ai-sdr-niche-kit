# Medical billing companies serving independent practices

**Default rank 36 of 50. Total 71 out of 100.** Health. Regulated: health. Depends on US law or US programs.

Scores are judgment scores against [RUBRIC.md](../RUBRIC.md), not market data.

## Who the buyer is

Owner of a small medical billing service, often a former practice manager.

## Who the booked calls are with

Practice managers and physician owners at independent clinics.

## Why they would pay for booked calls

A billing client stays for years and pays monthly. Owners are heads down on claims, and practices are hard to reach without someone persistent.

## A ready offer

I book calls with practice managers at independent clinics in your specialty. No patient information is ever touched by me or asked for in outreach.

## Opening message

Replace every part in square brackets with something true you found yourself. Never send it with a detail you made up.

> [First name], since you focus on [real specialty], you know how much a denied claim costs an independent practice in staff time. I can book calls for you with practice managers in that specialty, and I never touch patient data. Would you like to see how I would pick the clinics?

## Where to find the leads

- Healthcare billing association member lists where public
- Google Maps for medical billing services
- For their customers: the NPI Registry for practice names and clinic websites for manager names

## What to avoid

- Quoting a collection rate or revenue lift
- Asking for or looking at any patient record
- Pay per patient or per claim arrangements for the outreach

## Compliance note

A company that handles patient information for a provider, including billing, is a business associate under HIPAA (45 CFR 160.103). Stay out of patient data entirely. Do not state any improvement in collections unless the client can prove it. Payment tied to patient referrals can raise problems under the federal Anti Kickback Statute (42 U.S.C. 1320a-7b). This is not legal advice.
