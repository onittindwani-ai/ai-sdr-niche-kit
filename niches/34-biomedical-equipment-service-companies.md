# Independent biomedical equipment service companies

**Default rank 34 of 50. Total 72 out of 100.** Health. Regulated: health. The idea works in most countries, though the named lead sources are often US ones.

Scores are judgment scores against [RUBRIC.md](../RUBRIC.md), not market data.

## Who the buyer is

Owner of a small independent service company that repairs and maintains clinical equipment.

## Who the booked calls are with

Practice administrators, surgery center managers and biomedical leads at small hospitals.

## Why they would pay for booked calls

Service contracts renew and each customer has many devices. Owners are technicians first and are competing against manufacturer service plans.

## A ready offer

I book calls with surgery center managers and practice administrators to compare their service coverage, so your technicians get in front of the right person.

## Opening message

Replace every part in square brackets with something true you found yourself. Never send it with a detail you made up.

> [First name], the manufacturer's service plan is often the only option a surgery center hears about, so a capable independent like [their company] rarely gets a look. I can book those comparison calls for you. Would it help if I sent a list of centers near your techs?

## Where to find the leads

- Independent service organization lists and biomedical association members where public
- Google Maps for biomedical equipment repair
- For their customers: the NPI Registry, state surgery center licence lists and clinic websites

## What to avoid

- Claiming any device is unsafe or out of compliance
- Handling anything from equipment that stores patient data
- Saying the firm is authorized by a manufacturer without proof

## Compliance note

Servicing medical devices carries regulatory and manufacturer limits. Do not make safety or compliance claims about a device. If a device holds patient data, the service company must handle it under its own privacy agreements, and you should stay out of it. This is not legal advice.
