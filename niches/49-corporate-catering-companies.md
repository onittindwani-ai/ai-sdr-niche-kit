# Corporate catering companies

**Default rank 35 of 50. Total 71 out of 100.** Business services. Not in a regulated group. The idea works in most countries, though the named lead sources are often US ones.

Scores are judgment scores against [RUBRIC.md](../RUBRIC.md), not market data.

## Who the buyer is

Owner of a local catering business that wants regular office lunches rather than one off weddings.

## Who the booked calls are with

Office managers and executive assistants at offices and professional firms.

## Why they would pay for booked calls

Regular office orders are steadier than weddings. The owner is in the kitchen, and office managers are not looking for a new caterer until they get tired of the current one.

## A ready offer

I book tasting calls with office managers near your kitchen who order lunch for meetings every week.

## Opening message

Replace every part in square brackets with something true you found yourself. Never send it with a detail you made up.

> [First name], your [real dish or event] looks great, and office lunches could keep your kitchen busy on quiet weekdays. I find the office managers who order regularly near you and book a quick tasting call. Should I start with the offices closest to your kitchen?

## Where to find the leads

- Google Maps for caterers
- Local catering listings and business association members
- For their customers: office park tenant lists and professional firm sites

## What to avoid

- Food allergy or dietary promises
- Caterers with no capacity on weekdays
- Pitching only on price
