# Commercial landscape maintenance firms for HOAs and business parks

**Default rank 29 of 50. Total 73 out of 100.** Commercial trades. Not in a regulated group. The idea works in most countries, though the named lead sources are often US ones.

Scores are judgment scores against [RUBRIC.md](../RUBRIC.md), not market data.

## Who the buyer is

Owner of a landscape maintenance company with a few crews, trying to replace small residential accounts with larger contracts.

## Who the booked calls are with

Community association managers, HOA board members and business park managers.

## Why they would pay for booked calls

A single association contract can replace many small residential stops. Those contracts go through managers who rebid on a schedule, and the owner rarely knows when that window opens.

## A ready offer

I find community association managers near your crews and book calls ahead of their contract renewals, so you are in the room when the bid comes up.

## Opening message

Replace every part in square brackets with something true you found yourself. Never send it with a detail you made up.

> [First name], I noticed [real property or crew detail] on your site. Association contracts tend to be decided by one manager at renewal time, and I set up that first conversation well before then. Would it help if I showed you which managers run communities near your routes?

## Where to find the leads

- Google Maps for commercial landscaping by region
- NALP and state landscape association member pages where public
- For their customers: Community Associations Institute chapter directories where public, and management company staff pages

## What to avoid

- Mow and blow operators who cannot carry a large site
- Contacting individual homeowners in an association
- Any pitch that ignores existing contract terms
