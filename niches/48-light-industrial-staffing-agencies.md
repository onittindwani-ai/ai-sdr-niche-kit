# Light industrial staffing agencies

**Default rank 46 of 50. Total 65 out of 100.** Business services. Not in a regulated group. The idea works in most countries, though the named lead sources are often US ones.

Scores are judgment scores against [RUBRIC.md](../RUBRIC.md), not market data.

## Who the buyer is

Owner of a local staffing agency that fills warehouse and factory roles.

## Who the booked calls are with

Plant managers, warehouse managers and HR leads.

## Why they would pay for booked calls

Each client can bring recurring placements. Agencies have recruiters but are often short on someone opening new client accounts.

## A ready offer

I book calls with plant and warehouse managers who are hiring for roles you already fill, using their public job posts as the reason to reach out.

## Opening message

Replace every part in square brackets with something true you found yourself. Never send it with a detail you made up.

> [First name], you already fill roles like [real role] in [area], and I keep seeing warehouses posting for the same jobs. I book calls with those hiring managers for you. Could I send over this week's openings near you?

## Where to find the leads

- American Staffing Association member lists where public
- Google Maps and state staffing licence lists where they exist
- For their customers: public job boards, filtered by roles and location

## What to avoid

- Agencies that already have a business development team
- Promising hires within a set time
- Implying the agency handles employment law for the client

## Compliance note

Employment agencies are licensed in some states, and hiring must follow anti discrimination rules. Avoid any wording about who the client would or would not hire.
