# EV charger installers for apartments and workplaces

**Default rank 38 of 50. Total 70 out of 100.** Property and built environment. Not in a regulated group. The idea works in most countries, though the named lead sources are often US ones.

Scores are judgment scores against [RUBRIC.md](../RUBRIC.md), not market data.

## Who the buyer is

Owner of an electrical company that has added EV charging installation for commercial sites.

## Who the booked calls are with

Apartment owners, HOA boards, workplace facility managers and small fleet operators.

## Why they would pay for booked calls

Site owners are still working out whether to add chargers, and the job is usually large once they decide. The installer needs someone having those early conversations.

## A ready offer

I book calls with apartment owners and facility managers who are looking at charging, so you can scope the site before they ask three other companies.

## Opening message

Replace every part in square brackets with something true you found yourself. Never send it with a detail you made up.

> [First name], apartment owners keep getting asked about charging, and most of them do not know where to start. I can find the ones near you and book a short scoping call. Interested in seeing which properties around [area] I would reach out to?

## Where to find the leads

- State electrical licence lookups combined with EV charger brand installer lists
- Google Maps for EV charger installation
- For their customers: apartment association members where public and office park management firms

## What to avoid

- Quoting rebates or tax credits that may have changed
- Promising revenue from charging
- Sites where the electrical service clearly cannot support chargers

## Compliance note

Incentive programs change often. Never state an incentive or tax credit in a message without checking the current official page that day.
