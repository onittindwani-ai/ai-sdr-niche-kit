# Managed IT providers for small offices

**Default rank 11 of 50. Total 79 out of 100.** Technology. Not in a regulated group. The idea works in most countries, though the named lead sources are often US ones.

Scores are judgment scores against [RUBRIC.md](../RUBRIC.md), not market data.

## Who the buyer is

Owner of a small managed IT company, usually a technical founder running a help desk.

## Who the booked calls are with

Owners and office managers at professional firms with roughly 10 to 100 staff.

## Why they would pay for booked calls

Monthly contracts are valuable and these founders are usually more comfortable fixing servers than selling. Referrals come slowly and growth stalls.

## A ready offer

I book calls with office managers at professional firms near you who are unhappy with their IT support. You run a short technical review on the call.

## Opening message

Replace every part in square brackets with something true you found yourself. Never send it with a detail you made up.

> [First name], your [real certification or specialty] stands out, but I would guess growth still comes down to referrals. I look for professional firms near you with no clear IT partner, and I book the call for you. Want to see the kinds of firms I would look for?

## Where to find the leads

- Vendor partner directories and Clutch style agency lists
- Google Maps for managed IT services
- For their customers: local law, accounting and engineering firm sites

## What to avoid

- Using fear about a breach as the opener
- Claiming to have scanned a prospect's systems
- Owners who already pay several lead generation vendors
