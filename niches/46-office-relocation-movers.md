# Commercial office relocation movers

**Default rank 30 of 50. Total 73 out of 100.** Logistics. Not in a regulated group. The idea works in most countries, though the named lead sources are often US ones.

Scores are judgment scores against [RUBRIC.md](../RUBRIC.md), not market data.

## Who the buyer is

Owner of a moving company that wants commercial moves instead of household work.

## Who the booked calls are with

Office managers, facility leads and commercial real estate brokers.

## Why they would pay for booked calls

Commercial moves are larger and scheduled in advance. The owner has trucks and crew, but the household side keeps them too busy to pursue offices.

## A ready offer

I book intro calls with commercial brokers and facility managers who know which companies are about to move.

## Opening message

Replace every part in square brackets with something true you found yourself. Never send it with a detail you made up.

> [First name], I noticed you handle [real commercial move or service]. Companies planning a move usually tell their broker long before they call a mover, so I book intros with those brokers and with facility managers near you. Can I show you who I would approach first?

## Where to find the leads

- FMCSA and state mover licence lookups
- Google Maps for commercial moving companies
- For their customers: commercial broker sites and news of local lease signings

## What to avoid

- Rewarding brokers for referrals without checking state rules
- Household moves, which rely on consumer contact
- Promising a move with no downtime

## Compliance note

Movers need the correct operating authority. Confirm the licence, and do not pay real estate brokers for referrals without checking their rules first.
