# Backflow testing and certification companies

**Default rank 27 of 50. Total 73 out of 100.** Commercial trades. Not in a regulated group. Written for the US, needs local rules and sources elsewhere.

Scores are judgment scores against [RUBRIC.md](../RUBRIC.md), not market data.

## Who the buyer is

Owner of a small backflow testing business, often a plumber who holds the tester certification.

## Who the booked calls are with

Property managers, HOA managers and restaurant and retail groups with many water connections.

## Why they would pay for booked calls

Tests repeat on a cycle set by the local water utility, so a property manager with many buildings means a route of renewals. The owner is out testing, not making calls.

## A ready offer

I book calls with property managers who hold many water connections, so your renewals grow in blocks instead of one address at a time.

## Opening message

Replace every part in square brackets with something true you found yourself. Never send it with a detail you made up.

> [First name], I know backflow tests come back on a set cycle, so a manager with many buildings is worth more than any single address. I can book calls with those managers near you. Could I send a short list of the ones I found around [city]?

## Where to find the leads

- Water utility lists of approved backflow testers, which many utilities publish
- State plumbing licence lookups
- For their customers: property management company pages and HOA management firms

## What to avoid

- Sounding like an official notice from the water utility
- Very small one person testers with no capacity for more routes
- Stating a due date you cannot verify

## Compliance note

Tester certification and test cycles are set locally. Never imitate a utility notice or imply a prospect is overdue.
