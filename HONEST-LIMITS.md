# Honest limits

Please read this before you act on anything in the kit.

## The scores

- Every score is a **judgment score** against [the rubric](RUBRIC.md). One person scored all 50 from general knowledge of how these businesses work. They are not survey results or market data, and nobody measured them.
- A high rank is not a promise. It says the niche looks good on these 7 criteria, using these weights.
- A gap of a few points means nothing. Treat niches within about 5 points of each other as tied, and let your own knowledge decide.
- The experience bonus is a rule we chose (one matching tag adds 10 points, two or more add 15), because knowing the niche matters. It is not a measured effect.
- The niches were chosen by hand. Many good niches are not here, and the list leans towards US style small businesses.

## What the kit does not contain

- No market sizes, deal values, response rates, conversion rates, or earnings. We do not have real numbers, so we did not write any.
- No proof that any niche works. The kit has not been used to run a live campaign in these niches.
- No lead lists. It tells you where to look, and you check the terms of each source yourself.

## Laws and rules

- This is not legal advice. The compliance notes are warnings to go and check. A niche without a note can still have rules that apply.
- 16 niches are marked regulated (health, finance, legal). Pay per booked call can be restricted or illegal in those, especially where referrals are involved. Use a flat fee and get professional review.
- Only some rule pages could be opened when this was checked. [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md) says exactly which ones.
- Outside the US, rules can be very different. The tool leaves out niches that depend on US programs, and lowers those written around US sources, but it cannot tell you what is legal where you are.

## Other limits

- Product facts about Salesforce, HubSpot and Clay were checked on 2026-11-09 and can change quickly. Hunter's availability date in particular is unconfirmed.
- "Software someone inside the company still has to set up and run" is our opinion.
- The lead sources were named from general knowledge and not opened one by one. Most are US sources, so outside the US you will need the local equivalent.
- The opening messages are starting points. Each has parts you must fill in with something true, and you must add any legal footer your country requires.
- The worked examples are invented people, labelled synthetic. They show how the tool is used, and nothing else.
- `tests/check_kit.py` checks the arithmetic, the files and the tool. It cannot check whether a score is right.
- The kit was tested on Python 3.9 and 3.14 on a Mac. It was not tested on Windows, where the command may be `python` instead of `python3`.
