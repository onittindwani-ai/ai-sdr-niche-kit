# Why sell booked calls and not a tool

Facts below were checked on 2026-11-09 and are listed with links in [SOURCES.md](../SOURCES.md). These products change quickly, so check the vendor pages again before you repeat any of it.

## What the big companies have built

| Company | Product | What the company says (vendor statements) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Salesforce | Hunter, an outbound sales agent | Works a sales pipeline from research to outreach, under rules a sales team sets. Salesforce's September 2026 announcement said it was in pilot, with general availability planned for November 2026. |
| HubSpot | Prospecting Agent | Recommends leads for outreach. HubSpot says you pay $1 for each lead the agent recommends outreach for. Available in its Starter, Professional and Enterprise tiers, and it runs on HubSpot Credits. |
| Clay | Inbound SDR Agent | Announced in Clay's October 2026 recap of its Sculpt event, with a waitlist. |

One conflict to know about: a June 2026 Salesforce page called Hunter generally available, while the later September pages said pilot with November availability. We found no page confirming the date. Check it yourself before you state it.

## The gap (this part is our opinion)

Our opinion: all 3 are software that someone inside the company still has to set up and run. Salesforce's own wording talks about reps setting the goal and keeping control. That works if you have a sales team.

A business owner with no sales team has nobody to do that. A machine shop owner running the floor is not going to set up an outbound agent, write the rules, watch the results and fix what breaks.

That is the opening. The owner does not want an AI SDR. The owner wants a call on the calendar with someone who might buy.

## So what do you sell?

- **Not a tool.** Tools get compared to free trials and big brand names.
- **Booked calls.** A call with a real prospect is something an owner can understand and judge.
- **In 1 niche you know.** Knowing the niche is what lets you write messages that sound like you have been inside the business, and tell a good lead from a bad one.

Knowing a niche is not automatically enough. Some niches are a poor fit no matter what you know. That is what the [rubric](../RUBRIC.md) and the [leaderboard](../LEADERBOARD.md) are for.

## What this does not mean

It does not mean the big products are bad, or that a small service will beat them. It does not mean this is easy, or that it will make money. Test it with the [10 conversation test](TEN-CONVERSATION-TEST.md) before you commit.
